**Tweet 2 (The Problem):**  
Liquid cooling (DLC) handles 100kW+ racks at 60°C. But dumping 90MW of heat into the atmosphere in summer requires massive fans (~4MW) or evaporative cooling (1.5 Billion liters of fresh water/year).  

Water scarcity is triggering regulatory moratoria worldwide.

---

**Tweet 3 (The Breakthrough):**  
Updraft towers historically failed at power generation due to 2–5% Carnot limits.  

Flip the logic: Don't view it as a power plant. View it as a **massive passive natural-draft cooler**.  

The mountain slope provides the 1,500m chimney structure for free.

---

**Tweet 4 (The Math):**  
At 60°C waste heat, the density gradient drives ~4,480 kg/s of airflow via thermosiphon effect.  

- Fans required: **ZERO**  
- Water used: **ZERO**  
- Base turbines generate: **+2.25 MWel Net Power**  
- **PUE drops to < 1.01**

**Tweet 5 (The Economics):**

Saving 4MW fan power + generating 2.25MW + 0 water saves **>$10.1M/year in OPEX**.
