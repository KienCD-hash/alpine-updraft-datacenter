> Liquid cooling (Direct Liquid Cooling - DLC) for Nvidia Blackwell / H100 clusters outputs waste heat at ~60°C. But rejecting 90MWth of continuous heat during summer requires either massive fan arrays (~4MW parasitic load) or evaporative cooling (~1.58 Billion liters of freshwater/year for a 100MW site).
>
> Historically, Solar Updraft Towers failed as standalone power generators because their thermodynamic efficiency is capped at 2–5%. 
>
> We realized that flipping the paradigm changes everything: don't treat the updraft tower as a power plant. Treat it as a **massive, zero-water passive natural-draft cooler**.
>
> Key Findings from our paper:

> - **Base Turbines:** Recover +2.25 MWel of electricity from the rising air stream.
